CASE STUDY: META CONTENT MARKETING & SCALE

How Giga Digital Agency transformed an established fashion and lifestyle retail brand into an e-commerce powerhouse through full-funnel Meta advertising.

Retail & E-Commerce Flagship

Scaling From High Ad Waste to 4.8x ROAS

The Challenge: The client suffered from high Cost Per Click (CPC) and high cart abandonment (over 74%), with generic boosted posts failing to convert followers into paying customers.

Our Strategy: We restructured the brand's Meta ad account from scratch. We integrated Meta Conversions API (CAPI), produced 45+ dynamic video reels highlighting product features, and introduced automated retargeting discounts for abandoned checkouts.

The Execution: Over a 90-day execution period, our media buyers continuously tested 4 audience segments across Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, and overseas Bangladeshi expats, deploying one-click bKash payment landing pages.
